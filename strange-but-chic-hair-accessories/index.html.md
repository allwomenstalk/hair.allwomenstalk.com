# 10 Strange but Chic Hair Clips and Accessories to Refresh Your Look

I absolutely love hair accessories. Clips, pins, barrettes, bobby pins, little hats, scarves – you name it, I love it, and I probably have one or more hiding around the house. I've got accessories everywhere, some never worn, some worn constantly, others just waiting for that perfect outfit. I'm especially fond of unusual things, little items you might not expect. From eye-catching headbands to statement [chic hair clips](#7), there are so many fun ways to showcase your personality through your hairstyle. In the spirit of that fondness, here are some strange but chic hair accessories you might like, too!

## 1. Juicy Couture “Peace Hearts” Elastic Hair Bands

![Juicy Couture “Peace Hearts” Elastic Hair Bands](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ak/w3/maz9jrjj54c92c01e0ae8578309496_300x230.jpg)

**Price:** $25.00 at [Nordstrom](https://www.nordstrom.com)
These elastics aren't completely strange, but they're definitely unique and certainly chic. If there's one thing I love about Juicy Couture, it's the way they do accessories, from hair bands to handbags. The charms on these elastics are too cute for words – especially if you're all about love and peace! They add an instant touch of personality to a simple ponytail, much like styling with colorful [chic hair clips](#7) for a quick style boost.

## 2. Mad Tea Party Headband

_ **Price:** $115.00 at shanalogic.com_
This is by far the best strange but chic hair accessory I've ever come across. Sure, this is a little pricey for a headband, but if you're a ginormous Alice in Wonderland and Mad Hatter fan – like me! – then it seems more than fair. This is so cool, I think I'd give up buying books for a month just to have it. The way that teacup perches is pure art! And yeah, the Queen of Hearts design is delightfully whimsical, making it an irresistible statement piece for tea parties and costume events alike.

## 3. Diva Lila Hat with Veil

_ **Price:** $45.00 at shanalogic.com_
I'm a huge fan of hats, although my taste normally runs toward fedoras and classic headwear. However, there's something so saucy and sassy about this hat that I want it right now! I love the silver stars accenting it, and that delicate veiling is absolutely gorgeous. If you want to add a little mystery and old-school glamour to your evening look, this piece is an ideal choice, perfectly complimenting retro ensembles alongside [vintage-inspired hair clips](#8).

## 4. Peacock Feather Headband

![Peacock Feather Headband](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ps/ut/gv3ae48j54c92c046d725932985435_168x298.jpg)

**Price:** $5.80 at [Forever 21](https://www.forever21.com)
If you've ever felt pretty as a peacock, then this is definitely the strange but chic hair accessory you need to show it! I adore peacock feathers, and this one is particularly gorgeous. All that deep blue hue is so pretty, and for a change, I love the way the rich gold accents it. With all the colors, sequins, and beads, you'll mimic the majestic peacock effortlessly.

Key elements that make feather headbands stand out:

- Vibrant natural tones that pop against any hair color

- Intricate beadwork and sequin embellishments

- Lightweight design suitable for all-day wear

## 5. Nordstrom Ribbon Sun Hat

![Nordstrom Ribbon Sun Hat](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hi/br/ftwffzln54c92c054e7bd243108953_300x231.jpg)

**Price:** $23.90 at [Nordstrom](https://www.nordstrom.com)
This summer, if you worry about sun exposure, why not try a stylish sunhat? Why not, in particular, try a big, bold sunhat like this ribbon-trimmed option? It's so jaunty and colorful! Maybe not entirely strange, but it's definitely high fashion and chic. That wide silhouette, especially, takes it a step above ordinary summer headwear.

## 6. Classic Cinema Night Headband

![Classic Cinema Night Headband](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/q3/oq/jf7s51lz54c92c063003b913494719_210x299.jpg)

**Price:** $34.99 at [ModCloth](https://www.modcloth.com)
I absolutely adore little mini-hat headbands like this. Since the piece is attached to a comfortable band, you can wear it at a jaunty, angled tilt without worrying about it slipping. It's quirky, cool, strange, and chic all at once. How could you ask for anything more than that in a headpiece?

## 7. Super Sprinkles Hair Clips

_ **Price:** $8.00 at shanalogic.com_
If you love sweet, colorful aesthetics, look no further than these adorable [chic hair clips](#7). They look remarkably realistic – like you could pop in a miniature dime and get a handful of colorful gumballs! If you want a cheerful, playful vibe, these clips are all you need to elevate a basic outfit.

## 8. Kentucky Dainty Hair Clip

![Kentucky Dainty Hair Clip](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ro/wa/g4r81sj854c92c07c5a53222112589_210x295.jpg)

**Price:** $15.99 at [ModCloth](https://www.modcloth.com)
One of the highlights of Kentucky Derby season is the fashion – specifically the elaborate hats. If you want to channel that classic race-day spirit in a smaller, more wearable package, this hair clip is perfect for you! Unlike traditional headbands, this clip can be positioned anywhere in your hair using sturdy alligator clips for extra support. The delicate lace and floral detail make it truly special.

## 9. Mod Dots Bucket Hat

_ **Price:** $48.00 at shanalogic.com_
This retro mod hat is a gorgeous, strange but chic hair accessory that adds instant flair to any streetwear look. I love the mod geometric aesthetic, and although the bright dots on this bucket hat look minimalist, they make a strong visual statement. The shape and structured silhouette are flattering on every face shape. It takes a confident woman to pull off a bold bucket hat, but once you try it, you'll love the result!

## 10. Panache Pinto Hair Clip

![Panache Pinto Hair Clip](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tm/sy/tkyu9jiq54c92c096c726576402556_210x300.jpg)

**Price:** $11.99 at [ModCloth](https://www.modcloth.com)
Finally, if you love equestrian motifs or whimsical accessories, you've got to check out this pony hair clip. How cute is it? Beyond its charm, it is beautifully molded with detailed sculpting and etching that resembles a miniature figurine. Offered in vibrant magenta, it brings a playful pop of color to any hairstyle.
