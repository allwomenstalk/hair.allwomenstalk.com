# Why your transitioning hair routine isn’t working and how to fix it

If you are moving from a relaxed style into transitioning hair and going natural, you've got to see this list of tutorials below. Whether you are just starting out or you are hitting your first year of being natural remember, there are tons of different hair care tips that you need to watch out for! Building a reliable step-by step curly hair routine helps protect your delicate line of demarcation, making learning how to wash transitioning hair and detangle without breakage so much easier. Here are transitioning hairstyles for beginners and essential care techniques to keep your locks thriving.

- Keep your scalp clean and deeply hydrated

- Minimize high-heat manipulation on fragile ends

- Detangle gently using wide-tooth combs and high-slip conditioners

## 1. Bantu Knots Hair Tutorial

These knots are so, so cool and look amazing. Bantu knots are a classic protective style that blends your relaxed ends with your natural curl pattern while shielding fragile strands. If you prefer heat-free definition, try our [heat-free stretched curls](#3) technique for low-manipulation styling that holds moisture all week long.

## 2. Natural Hair Care Cutting, Step-by-step

If you're looking for a way to cut your natural hair, this is the best way to do it! Trimming damaged relaxed ends gradually—or opting for a big chop—prevents split ends from traveling up the hair shaft. Regular dusting of dead ends keeps your texture balanced as your new growth comes in strong.

## 3. Heat Free Stretched Curls

OMG, these curls are absolutely beautiful and easy to do. Stretching your texture without flat irons protects your delicate hair shaft from heat damage. Using high-moisture leave-ins like those from [Pattern Beauty](https://www.patternbeauty.com) helps preserve elasticity while safely elongating your curls.

## 4. Washing Your Transitioning Hair

This is a fantastic tutorial on how to wash your transitioning hair! Knowing how to wash transitioning hair properly prevents severe tangling at the line of demarcation. Pair your wash session with a nourishing [deep conditioning treatment](#7) to restore moisture balance and elasticity after cleansing.

## 5. Natural Hair Regimen

The cool thing about this tutorial is it is step-by-step and easy to follow. Establishing a consistent weekly care routine eliminates guesswork and helps track hair health progress over time.

## 6. Blow-drying Transitioning Hair

Want to straighten your natural hair? This is how to do it! When blow-drying, always apply a high-quality thermal protectant and use a tension comb attachment on low to medium heat settings to keep fragile ends intact.

## 7. The DEEP Conditioning Treatment

If you are dealing with frizz, a deep conditioner is needed. Follow along with this great tutorial! Using a dedicated deep conditioner for transitioning hair once a week infuses essential hydration into dry strands. Rich formulas from brands like [SheaMoisture](https://www.sheamoisture.com) help smooth cuticles and reduce stubborn frizz effectively.

## 8. How to Detangle Your Transitioning Hair

Promise, this is easy to follow and so needed. Learning how to detangle transitioning hair requires patience, ample slip from conditioner, and working in small sections from ends to roots to prevent unnecessary breakage.

## 9. Beautiful Braid out

Take a look for how to do a braid out for your transitioning hair. Braid outs provide uniform wave patterns that effortlessly hide differing textures between new growth and relaxed ends.

## 10. Transitioning Hair Tips – Best Ever!

These tips will help you think about transitioning. Finding versatile easy hairstyles for transitioning hair keeps your daily routine stress-free while your natural texture grows out strong and healthy.

## 11. Full Curly Hair Routine

From wash to dry! The hair looks beautiful too, seriously. Mastering a complete step-by-step curly hair routine ensures your strands receive adequate hydration, definition, and protection every single day.

## 12. Wash&Go Routine

Wash and go! You'll see just how easy it is. Adapting a wash and go transitioning hair method using defined gels from [Carol's Daughter](https://www.carolsdaughter.com) brings out your natural curl pattern without heat stress.

## 13. Fluffy Wand Curls

These curls are beautiful and the hair is all natural! Wand curls offer voluminous, bouncy body whenever you want a special occasion look with heat-defined texture.

## 14. The Winter Routine

You should be changing around your routine from summer, spring, fall and finally winter. Cold weather strips moisture quickly, so heavier butter creams like those from [Cantu Beauty](https://www.cantubeauty.com) provide necessary seasonal protection against harsh weather.

- Switch to rich, oil-based sealants

- Protect hair under silk or satin lined scarves and bonnets

- Deep condition weekly with gentle warmth or steam

## 15. Post-wash

What should you be doing after you wash all of your beautiful hair? Well, check out this tutorial! Knowing how to style transitioning hair after washing ensures moisture stays locked inside before you diffuse or air dry. For a quick low-fuss style, consider switching to a [wash and go routine](#12) that keeps your curls hydrated.

## 16. How You Can Straighten Your Hair

And it'll look beautiful. Straightening natural or dual-textured hair safely requires clean strands, lightweight heat protectant oils, and single-pass silk press techniques.

## 17. Shampoo Those Strands in Braids

Shampoo, oil and some water is all you'll need! Washing your hair while in braids or twists keeps your scalp fresh and clean while minimizing friction and tangling at the roots.
