# 7 Amazing Products for Stubborn Hair: How to Fix & Style It

If you have ever asked yourself why is my hair so stubborn or wondered how to deal with stubborn hair when every routine seems to fail, you are not alone. I know how frustrating finding the right products for stubborn hair can be. Some people say you get what you pay for, assuming higher price tags mean superior results. I totally disagree after trying endless solutions to cure my dry, brittle, hard-to-grow, and straight yet kinky hair. I have incredibly thick hair, which I love, but on a bad hair day, it can be a nightmare! It used to break and shed something terrible, and waiting for it to grow felt like waiting for Christmas as a kid. My hair used to grow only about an inch every few months, and for my impatient self, that just wouldn't cut it. After extensive testing, I have figured out how to fix stubborn hair and found dependable products that help it grow while keeping it shiny, moisturized, silky, and strong. Here are my top 7 favorite products for stubborn hair, which work wonders to leave you with more spare time and more hair on your head instead of in your brush or on the floor!

I’ve tried many, but the [Sundown Naturals hair skin and nails](https://www.cvs.com/) formula is my absolute favorite. It is packed with essential vitamins and nutrients that keep hair strong, shiny, help it stop breaking, and most importantly, support steady growth. Key benefits of adding a daily beauty supplement to your regimen:

- Supports hair follicle integrity to minimize daily breakage
- Promotes natural growth rate and enhances strand elasticity
- Provides essential nutrients for healthier skin and stronger nails

## 2. Jojoba Oil

![Jojoba Oil](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/08/30035849_9618_599x598.jpg)

If you have dry or brittle hair like I do, you’ll like this product a lot! This natural oil is full of nourishing ingredients that keep hair from breaking and help it maintain its vibrant shine. To apply this oil, place a small amount on your hair before showering and rub it in thoroughly from roots to ends. Pin your hair on top of your head so it stays out of the way. Then, hop in the shower and wash your face and body first. The moisture and steam humidity from the shower will increase the effectiveness of the oil and help your strands absorb deep hydration. Afterwards, take down your hair, then wash and condition as normal. You’ll notice as you comb out your hair that it isn’t only stronger, but much easier to comb through, silkier, and it dries beautifully. You can also check our [hair care guides](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/) for more natural oil treatments. I don’t even need heavy styling agents on the days I use jojoba oil, which saves me money and keeps my hair feeling light!

## 3. Nourishing Hair Spray

![Nourishing Hair Spray](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/08/30035846_6545_299x292.jpg)

If you can’t go without hair spray, don’t worry; you don’t have to give it up! The trick to finding the right product to keep hair in place is choosing a formula that won’t dry your hair out, cause breakage, or leave a crunchy texture. [Giovanni Cosmetics](https://giovannicosmetics.com/) is a brand I trust because they make high-quality beauty products that retain moisture and deliver essential botanical nutrients. This nourishing hair spray is a hair product that doesn't make hair hard, making it perfect when I create an updo or a hairstyle that needs reliable control all day. It leaves my hair super soft while keeping every strand locked in position without sticky or harsh chemicals.

## 4. A Great Leave in Treatment

![A Great Leave in Treatment](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/08/30035849_8644_599x571.jpg)

On days I don’t want to wash my hair, I always like to still condition it to preserve shine and prevent breakage. If you have fine or oily-prone hair, you can use a lightweight [leave in treatment](#4) right out of the shower instead of a rich pre-shower oil like jojoba oil. A fantastic leave-in treatment I rely on is Giovanni’s Vitapro-Fusion Protective Moisture Leave-In. It is rich in protective nutrients that keep your hair smooth without creating unwanted grease. I spray this on dry or damp hair and comb through to achieve a silky texture. It won’t weigh your hair down and smells incredible too! You can even spray this leave-in treatment on wet hair right after showering to make detangling effortless and avoid breaking delicate wet strands.

## 5. Anti-Frizz Agent

![Anti-Frizz Agent](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/08/30035849_1042_600x600.jpg)

If unexpected humidity or frizz makes your hair a wreck, learning how to style stubborn hair with a quality anti-frizz spray can make all the difference. A stylist once applied a heavy anti-frizz spray on my hair that left it stiff and hard to manage, which made me hesitant to try anti-frizz products for a long time. However, after trying the lightweight anti-frizz serum by Giovanni, I was amazed by the transformation. Formulated without harsh synthetic chemicals, a light mist on difficult hair tames humidity in minutes. It keeps stubborn strands under control while restoring a healthy sheen. Try spraying a light mist a few minutes before finishing your final styling details for a polished, frizz-free finish.

## 6. A Hair Rinse

![A Hair Rinse](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/08/30035849_9213_599x597.jpg)

I’ve always heard that diluted apple cider vinegar makes a great clarifying hair rinse to remove excess product build-up from hair and scalp, but raw ACV often left my hair feeling overly dry. I discovered a specialized clarifying formula by [WiseWays Herbals](https://wiseways.com/) that combines organic apple cider vinegar with soothing herbs to prevent dryness. It works as an effective hair product remover that boosts natural shine when applied before shampooing. It effectively removes stubborn residue and calms flaky scalp caused by changing weather. They even offer customized formulations for lighter and darker hair to protect your natural color tone.

Simple guidelines for using a clarifying hair rinse effectively:

- Apply directly to the scalp and lengths prior to shampooing
- Use once every 1 to 2 weeks to prevent product build-up
- Rinse out with lukewarm water to preserve natural hair oils

Dealing with dry hair and an itchy scalp can be a real struggle, especially if you experience dry scalp alongside oily roots at the same time! Finding the right balance can be confusing, which is why I love the Hair and Scalp Cream by WiseWays Herbals.
