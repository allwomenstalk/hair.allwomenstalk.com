# 9 Iconic Celebrity Hairstyles to Try for Your Next Look

Celebrity hairstyles come and go with passing trends, but some famous hairstyles remain timelessly iconic. There are certain iconic celebrity hairstyles you remember long after the movie has become a classic, or long after someone else has popularized a different look. If you are looking to try celebrity hairstyles for a fresh new look, checking out these legendary cuts is a great place to start! Here are 9 famous celebrity hairstyles to try on for your next hair transformation.

## 1. Louise Brooks' Bob

![Louise Brooks' Bob](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ks/px/f448hnwn54c9334c0d9bc096887769_400x239.jpg)

Silent film icon [Louise Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louise_Brooks) has one of the most signature celebrity hairstyles of all time. If you aren't into today's sharp, modern angled bobs, think about this classic flapper cut with blunt fringe. It will certainly give your look a sophisticated vintage flair that never goes out of fashion.

## 2. Meg Ryan's Short Shag

![Meg Ryan's Short Shag](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ch/r1/ws4bgxr154c9334cd58a6109461647_359x313.jpg)

I really love Meg Ryan's hair – especially when it's short, textured, and delightfully messy. If you're not afraid of layers and want something short that isn't quite a pixie crop, consider this beachy, playful look. It's effortless to pull off, easy to care for, and simple to style with a bit of texturizing spray – what could be better?

- Great for fine to medium hair textures looking for instant volume
- Requires minimal styling time—just toss with texturizing pomade

## 3. Halle Berry's Shorn Locks

![Halle Berry's Shorn Locks](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/nk/p6/hm5egu3154c9334e1170d266929780_400x268.jpg)

However, if you do want to go short, there are several famous hairstyles you can try. Oscar winner [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry)'s signature shorn look is among my absolute favorites. When you want to highlight a long neck, gorgeous eyes, and striking cheekbones, this chic crop is the way to go. If you are comparing shorter cuts, take a look at [Mia Farrow's crop](#7) or [Audrey Hepburn's pixie](#9) for similar inspiration.

## 4. Marilyn Monroe's Platinum Curls

![Marilyn Monroe's Platinum Curls](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ac/it/bojevz7754c9334eed9b8714014148_400x300.jpg)

Marilyn Monroe is an enduring style icon for a reason, and one of my favorite things about her remains her hair. She was just so remarkably, undeniably blonde. You never saw a dark root once Norma Jean transformed into Marilyn. If you're looking for a dramatic change, why not try platinum curls? Just make sure to keep your locks hydrated with rich hair masks!

## 5. Rita Hayworth's Long Red Locks

![Rita Hayworth's Long Red Locks](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ed/ol/voa40ay154c9334ff25b3170062498_400x312.jpg)

Who says you have to turn to modern stars to find the best iconic hairstyles? Golden Age Hollywood siren [Rita Hayworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rita_Hayworth) brought timeless glamour with her signature cascading red waves. Among all classic rita hayworth hairstyles, this fiery crimson shade stands out as pure Old Hollywood glamour. If you want to get a little daring, fiery, and luscious with your mane, a rita hayworth hairstyle is an unforgettable choice. You can also contrast this with [Marilyn Monroe's platinum curls](#4) for another legendary vintage look.

| Hairstyle Category | Key Feature | Best Face Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic Flapper Bob | Sharp perimeter & blunt bangs | Oval, Heart |
| Textured Short Shag | Piecey layers & effortless movement | Round, Oval |
| Vintage Glamour Waves | Soft voluminous waves & side part | Square, Diamond |

## 6. Victoria Beckham's Pageboy

![Victoria Beckham's Pageboy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ta/ce/lflq15uy54c9335100439026513838_400x285.jpg)

Victoria Beckham is a true hair trendsetter. While everyone once clamored for Jennifer Aniston's "The Rachel," Beckham's sharp, angled cuts became some of the most copied celebrity hairstyles worldwide. The look is edgy, flatters most facial shapes, and delivers an incredibly sleek, high-fashion finish.

## 7. Mia Farrow's Crop

![Mia Farrow's Crop](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d5/wr/g37tsrk954c93351df8a2922649710_400x270.jpg)

Mia Farrow's famous pixie cut in _Rosemary's Baby_ created by Vidal Sassoon is one of the most iconic celebrity hairstyles in film history. It is easy to see why fashion stylists remain obsessed with this cut. Much like [Halle Berry's shorn locks](#3), if you want to accent your facial features and high cheekbones, don't be afraid to go ultra-short!

## 8. Naomi Campbell's Long Tresses

![Naomi Campbell's Long Tresses](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cx/si/q6g63p9154c933532a733223730903_400x268.jpg)

It's not all about short haircuts, though. Supermodel Naomi Campbell has been rocking long, sleek, waist-length tresses for decades. If you have defined features, straight hair like this creates a striking, elegant frame. It takes dedicated maintenance and quality heat protection, but the sleek result is worth every minute.

- Use a flat iron paired with a high-shine serum for a glass-hair finish
- Apply heat protectant thoroughly to preserve length and health

## 9. Audrey Hepburn's Pixie

![Audrey Hepburn's Pixie](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/s9/hj/e6iwr0vf54c9335418f56750930176_400x258.jpg)

Still, sometimes short hair completely defines a signature look. Gorgeous as [Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn) looked with long hair – especially her famous Holly Golightly updos in _Breakfast at Tiffany's_ – her soft pixie cut truly showcased her delicate face. It's soft and micro-fringed, so if you want to transition into short hair gradually, take a page out of _Sabrina_'s book. You can compare this with [Louise Brooks' bob cut](#1) for another timeless vintage option.

Many celebrity hairstyles are transient trends, but these classic cuts remain forever stylish. Whether you love short cropped styles like Halle Berry's and Audrey Hepburn's or glamorous vintage waves like Rita Hayworth's, there is a legendary look for everyone to try. Which of these iconic celebrity hairstyles is your favorite?
