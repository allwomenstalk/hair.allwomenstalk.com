# 7 Best YouTube Channels for Hair and Top Hair Care YouTubers

It seems like there’s a YouTube channel for everything: funny cats, viral memes, and of course, movie trailers. But my favorite category of YouTube channels is all about hair! Whether you are searching for top hair care YouTubers, looking for step-by-step tutorial channels, or trying to find the best hair YouTubers for everyday styling, there are literally thousands of options online. Finding the best hair care youtube channels isn't always easy with so much content out there, but I can help! Here are my picks for the 7 best YouTube channels for hair—grab your comb, some bobby pins, and ponytail holders, and let’s dive into these top hairstyle YouTube channels!

## 1. Bebexo

I absolutely adore Bebexo; in my view, she runs one of the absolute best hair YouTube channels on the platform! She is completely gorgeous, insanely talented, and consistently creates some of the most innovative hair care tutorials online. Her video instructions are clear, simple, and detailed from start to finish so there is never any room for error. If you love discovering [intricate braiding methods](#5) and quick updos, her channel is an essential watch. Check out her official content on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/bebexo) to level up your styling game!

## 2. The Beauty Department

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/beautydept)
**Two words:** Lauren Conrad. I love her contributions to this project, which easily stands as one of the indisputable best hair care YouTube channels for beauty lovers. Her demo for the classic fishtail braid is iconic—she actually executes the braid herself on camera and makes the technique look completely effortless for viewers of any skill level.

## 3. Cute Girls’ Hairstyles

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/CuteGirlsHairstyles)
As the name of this hair YouTube channel implies, it’s all about cute, practical hairstyles for everyday wear! There is always something fresh and creative featured here, including unique styles you won’t easily find anywhere else. It’s the perfect channel to visit when you’re looking for quick morning routines or back-to-school hair inspiration!

Here are a few quick essentials featured across their popular tutorials:

- Heatless morning waves and quick accent braids
- Back-to-school ponytail variations
- Simple 5-minute styles for busy mornings

## 4. The Basics on Beauty

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/TheBasicsOnBeauty)
While many hair stylist YouTubers cater primarily to audiences in their 20s, this channel offers a fantastic variety of how-to tutorials for younger girls and beginners. The videos break down basic techniques with patient, clear instructions. They take plenty of time explaining each step, meaning you won't have to constantly rewind or pause to catch up—much like the detailed style breakdowns on [Bebexo's channel](#1).

## 5. Luxy Hair

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/LuxyHair)
Luxy Hair is consistently recognized as one of the best hair YouTubers and beauty channels because they offer hundreds of accessible tutorials for every hair type. Whether you want to master a simple French braid, try elaborate party updos, or learn proper hair care routines, their library has something for everyone. You can explore their full playlist library directly on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/LuxyHair).

Here is a quick breakdown of what makes these channels stand out for different styling needs:

| Channel Name | Core Focus | Experience Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bebexo | Creative Updos & Quick Styles | Beginner to Intermediate |
| Luxy Hair | Braids & Hair Extensions | All Skill Levels |
| Cute Girls’ Hairstyles | Easy Everyday Hairstyles | Beginner Friendly |

## 6. Foxy Locks Extensions

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/FoxyLocksExtensions)
If you have shorter hair or fine hair, this is a top-tier hair channel you definitely need to subscribe to! The channel provides endless creative ideas on how to seamlessly use clip-in extensions to transform your look, ranging from effortless messy ponytails to elegant updos and voluminous braid styles. For even more hair extension inspiration, visit their official page on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/FoxyLocksExtensions).

## 7. Wendy’s Lookbook

[youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/user/wendyslookbook)
Wendy brings impeccable fashion sense and aesthetic flair to her content, making her channel one of the best YouTube channels for hair and personal style. Her video tutorials are beautifully organized and demonstrate exactly how to pair specific hairstyles with complete outfits for any occasion. Head over to [Wendy's Lookbook on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/wendyslookbook) for top-tier visual guides!

With so many marvelous, helpful hair care YouTubers and hairstyle YouTube channels producing free guides, there’s no reason to ever leave the house without a stylish, gorgeous do! I especially love Bebexo, but which of these hair channels is your personal favorite? Or do you follow another hair stylist on YouTube that should have made this list? Please share!
