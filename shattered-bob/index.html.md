# The Shattered Bob Is Fall's Most Wearable Short Cut: What to Ask For and How to Style It

A shattered bob is a soft, textured bob that falls somewhere between the jaw and the shoulders, with slightly broken-up ends, subtle graduation and a heavier front, so it looks lived-in instead of sharp. To get one, ask for soft, varied layers and lightly broken ends, then style it with a light texturizer and very little heat.

## What is a shattered bob?

The shattered bob is the relaxed, undone version of the classic bob. Hairstylists Stephen Buller and Anita Rice describe it to [ELLE UK](https://www.elle.com/uk/beauty/hair/a74144294/the-shattered-bob-for-autumn/) as “jaw-to-shoulder lengths, slightly broken edges, subtle graduation and a heavier front.” It should feel “lived-in rather than perfectly placed, almost as though the haircut has naturally fallen into that shape.”

ELLE UK frames it as fall's answer to summer's sharp, blunt bob: the same short length, but with movement and texture instead of a crisp line. The magazine spotted the look at London Fashion Week, and it suits anyone who wants short hair without daily sculpting.

## What to ask your stylist for

Hair terms vary from salon to salon, so describe the result you want. Try this word for word:

“I'd like a jaw-to-shoulder bob with soft, varied layers and lightly broken-up, textured ends. Keep a bit of graduation and some weight at the front. I want it to look lived-in, not sharp or blunt.”

A few extras that help:

- **Bring photos of people with your hair type.** ELLE UK recommends this, so your stylist can adapt the cut to your texture and face shape. A wavy shattered bob on a straight-haired client won't look the same.

- **Ask how they'll texturise the ends.** Point cutting and razor cutting are both common. If your hair is fine or curly, ask whether they'd adjust the technique, because heavy texturising can thin out the ends.

- **Talk about your routine.** Tell them how long you actually spend on your hair each morning.

## Who does a shattered bob suit?

- **Wavy and curly hair:** ELLE UK says the cut works especially well here, because the layers enhance natural shape and movement.

- **Thick hair:** the texturising removes bulk, so the bob sits lighter and moves more.

- **Fine hair:** it can work, but keep the texturising light so the ends don't look thin or wispy. Ask your stylist for a slightly blunter base with only a little texture on top.

- **Straight hair:** it's doable with some texture spray or a few bends from a wand. Otherwise it may read closer to a classic bob.

## How to style a shattered bob in five minutes

Keep it light. Buller and Rice say the appeal is that the cut still feels polished “but it's being roughed up.”

- **Start with damp hair.** Work a small amount of texturising mousse or cream through the mid-lengths and ends, keeping product away from the roots.

- **Air-dry or rough-dry.** Scrunch with your hands, or blow-dry on low without a brush.

- **Add a few bends if you want more shape.** Wrap a few random sections around a wand or flat iron, leaving the ends out.

- **Finish with texture.** Mist on a dry texturizing spray and pull the ends apart with your fingers.

- **Leave it a little undone.** It shouldn't look perfect.

## Growing it out and maintenance

ELLE UK says the shattered bob is cut to “grow out seamlessly” and needs less day-to-day styling than a sharper bob. The soft, broken ends hide regrowth better than a crisp line. How often you need a trim depends on your hair type and how much shape you want to keep, so ask your stylist to suggest a schedule when you book.

## FAQ

### What is a shattered bob?

A shattered bob is a jaw-to-shoulder bob with slightly broken, textured ends, soft varied layers, subtle graduation and a heavier front. It's designed to look lived-in rather than sleek.

### What's the difference between a shattered bob and a blunt bob?

A blunt bob has one crisp line and a polished finish. A shattered bob has soft, broken-up edges and layers, needs less styling and tends to grow out more gracefully.

### What do I ask my hairdresser for?

Ask for “a jaw-to-shoulder bob with soft, varied layers and lightly broken-up, textured ends, a little graduation and weight kept at the front, so it looks lived-in, not blunt,” and bring photos of people with your hair type.

### Does a shattered bob work on fine, wavy or curly hair?

It's especially flattering on wavy and curly hair. Fine hair can work too, but ask your stylist to keep the texturising light so the ends don't look thin.

### How do you style a shattered bob?

Apply a light texturising mousse or cream to damp mid-lengths and ends, air-dry or rough-dry, add a few loose bends if you like, then finish with texture spray. Keep it minimal.
