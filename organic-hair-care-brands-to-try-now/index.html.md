# Why Switching to Organic Hair Care Brands Is Worth It: 7 Top Picks

I adore many of the organic hair care brands I've treated my very long, super-thick tresses to! Finding the best natural & organic hair products can feel like a trial-and-error process. Some have failed to condition and tame my crazy mane, but there are quite a few top rated organic hair products that I hit the jackpot with. If an organic hair care product can saturate my strands with moisture and manageability, then it can pretty much do it for any hair type on the planet. Here is a curated guide to 7 standout organic hair care lines to try now for healthier, happier hair.

When switching to clean beauty, looking for natural hair care brands that prioritize pure ingredients can make all the difference for your locks:

- **Plant-Based Botanicals:** Cold-pressed oils and natural extracts that nourish without heavy residues.
- **Sulfate-Free Cleansers:** Gentle formulas that clean without stripping natural oils.
- **Cruelty-Free Standards:** Ethical formulations crafted without synthetic dyes or artificial fragrances.

## 1. Carina Organics

This is one of my absolute favorite organic hair care brands. Their deep conditioner is hands down the best organic hair care product I've ever tried for deep hydration. Hand-made in North Vancouver, this father-and-son founded line produces organic shampoos, conditioners, and styling aids without a single synthetic dye or artificial fragrance. Instead, their formulas are packed with super-nourishing ingredients like coconut oil, green apple extract, and avocado oil. You can explore their full range on the official [Carina Organics site](https://carinaorganics.com). After using their line, I always see an instant difference. Softer, shinier hair that I can get my fingers through without a single catch is no small feat, and Carina Organics delivers every time!

## 2. Josh Rosebrook

A celebrated hairstylist, Josh Rosebrook took his appreciation for organic beauty and natural ingredients and molded it into an exclusive line of skin and hair care. Based in L.A., this high-end organic beauty brand is loved by Mila Kunis, Ellen DeGeneres, and other A-listers. It's easy to see why these products earn glowing reviews; they are formulated with the utmost integrity and highest quality ingredients, cutting no corners to produce the best organic hair care products that truly perform. If you check out [Josh Rosebrook's official collection](https://joshrosebrook.com), you must try their firm hold hairspray. Positive Josh Rosebrook firm hold hair spray reviews praise its foundation of vitamin-infused distilled water bolstered by non-GMO organic corn extract and rosemary essential oil. Don't let the natural ingredients fool you—it held my heavy, stubborn hair in ringlets for 8 full hours! It is undoubtedly one of the best natural & organic hair products on the market today.

## 3. Delizioso Skincare

This business is run by two lovely sisters in London, Ontario. Offering a complete collection of cosmetics, skincare, body care, and hair products, [Delizioso Skincare](https://deliziososkincare.com) uses only the most natural and nourishing elements and is devoted to complete ingredient transparency. Their Bergamot & Eucalyptus Anti-Frizz Treatment Oil adds incredible shine while taming frizz naturally. Another must-try is their Elderberry Hair Smoothie, ideal for deep penetration of dry, thirsty hair—delivering rich moisture much like [Carina Organics' deep conditioner](#1) does for coarse textures.

## 4. Nezza Naturals

Owned and operated by a father-daughter team in Victoria, B.C., Nezza Naturals churns out an amazing lineup of eco-conscious skin, body, and hair care items. They make one heck of an organic hair serum that restores shine without leaving a greasy residue, and their Patchouli & Orange conditioner is fantastic for reviving dry, damaged strands.

## 5. Yarok

Founded by renowned Israeli hairstylist Mordechai Alvow, this NYC-based line is committed to total ingredient transparency and using only the safest natural and organic ingredients. Available through [Yarok's official shop](https://yarokhair.com), their Feed Your Do Styling Whip allows you to shape and style effortlessly while enjoying the uplifting aromas of lemon peel and peppermint. It works wonderfully on its own or layered alongside a [soothing scalp treatment](#7) for ultimate hair health.

## 6. Bubble and Bee

By the time Bubble and Bee founder Stephanie Greenwood was 16, she was taking advanced Chemistry classes at the University of Utah. Upon discovering how many toxic chemicals were hidden in standard beauty products, she took it upon herself to develop a clean line using certified organic hair care ingredients. Trusted and loved worldwide, Bubble and Bee's hair care line receives constant praise. I have many friends with oily hair who swear by their Organic Lemon Shampoo for keeping scalp oils balanced! Their lineup also features organic lip balms, facial care, and body scrubs.

## 7. Graydon

This Toronto-based clean beauty brand boasts the amazing Hair Smoothie, which is armed with powerful, nutrient-rich broccoli seed oil. This natural gem gives hair a soft, silky texture without any synthetic silicones! If you have a sensitive scalp, try their amazing Lavender Rosemary Blood Orange Aromablend to soothe and feed your roots. If you need weightless hold after conditioning, pair it with [Josh Rosebrook's firm hold hairspray](#2) for a complete natural styling routine.
