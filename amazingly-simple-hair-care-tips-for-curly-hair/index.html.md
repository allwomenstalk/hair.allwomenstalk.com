# 7 Things You’re Probably Doing Wrong with Curly Hair Care \(and Simple Fixes\)

I love my curly mane. It took years of trial and error for me to get there, but today my natural curls are my absolute favorite feature. Along the road of love and hate, I’ve learned some practical curly hair care tips that completely transformed my relationship with my hair. Understanding essential hair care tips for curly hair can make all the difference in achieving healthy, defined spirals without constant frizz. Today, I’m excited to share these 7 amazingly simple hair care tips for curly hair that will help you embrace your texture and elevate your daily routine.

| Routine Step | Recommended Frequency | Core Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Layered Haircut | Every 8–12 Weeks | Distributes weight & prevents flat crown |
| Co-Washing / Low-Poo | 1–2 Times a Week | Retains natural scalp moisture |
| Deep Conditioning Mask | Weekly Treatment | Restores keratin strength & elasticity |

## 1. Layers!

Curly hair needs layers, and my curls always seem to fall flat or flare out into an unwanted triangle shape without them. I always ask my stylist to cut my layers shorter at the crown and at an angle around my face, as it really frames my face beautifully and gives my hair natural volume. In fact, layering is easily the single best curly hair care tip I ever received from a professional stylist. If you are struggling with weighed-down locks, I strongly suggest you give strategic layering a shot during your next salon visit.

## 2. Shampoo Less

Okay, so you are probably thinking, "What kind of hair care tip is this, anyways?" but just trust me on this one and you'll soon understand why it works so well. You see, curly hair inherently needs a lot of moisture to stay healthy and defined, so the best way to keep those gorgeous spirals shiny and bouncy is to shampoo less and condition a lot more. Frequent washing strips your hair of its natural oils, whereas adopting a gentler cleansing routine or [deep moisturizing](#3) strategy helps preserve crucial hydration.

## 3. Moisturize

Fight the frizz by letting pro stylists share their favorite curly hair care tip! Let your regular conditioner sit in your hair for about 5 minutes in the shower, thoroughly covering each strand from root to end. Rinse well with cool water and follow up by gently combing through a nourishing leave-in conditioner, such as those from [SheaMoisture](https://www.sheamoisture.com). Frizz and humidity won’t stand a chance against this double moisturizing punch!

## 4. Hair Mask

Keep up with your hydration routine by applying an intensive hair mask once a week. This is another essential hair care tip that made my curls feel amazing and got me to a point where I just can't help but love them. Consider using a quality keratin-based or protein-enriched mask, like treatments from [Olaplex](https://www.olaplex.com), because they work by sealing the hair cuticle and strengthening the shaft against daily heat and environmental damage. Combining a weekly mask with [shampooing less](#2) ensures long-lasting softness and resilience.

## 5. Air Dry

I know that a busy schedule and lack of free time often make this next hair care tip feel difficult, but whenever you have the time, let your curly hair dry naturally in the air. The direct heat from a blow dryer can cause major structural damage and dry out your delicate curls. Since dry curls tend to frizz uncontrollably, taking time for air-drying will give you a double gain: significantly healthier hair and a smoother, more defined hairstyle. Who wouldn't want that effortless combo?

Key benefits of letting your curls air dry include:

- Reduces heat damage and split ends
- Preserves natural moisture balance
- Enhances natural curl pattern definition

## 6. Diffuse

When heat drying is a must, always use a diffuser attachment. Simply speaking, a diffuser is that round, spiked attachment for your hairdryer that disperses stream airflow so it distributes heat gently instead of blasting direct hot air onto one section of hair. Premium tools like the [Dyson Supersonic diffuser](https://www.dyson.com) help hair dry evenly while keeping curl patterns intact. Whenever I use mine, I like to flip my hair over to one side and dry gently from the ends up toward my scalp for extra volume.

## 7. Bad Hair Days

So what simplehair tips are there for those unavoidable bad hair days? I’m so glad you asked! We all have days when our hair simply won’t cooperate, no matter what products we use. The great thing about curly hair is that it looks effortlessly stylish when pulled back and twisted into a loose pineapple bun or updo, leaving just a few playful curls out to frame your face.

I hope other readers with curly hair will give these simple hair care tips a try and fall in love with their natural spirals as well. But remember, curl care is a personal journey!
