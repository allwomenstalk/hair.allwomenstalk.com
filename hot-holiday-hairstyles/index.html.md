# 7 Sexy Holiday Hairstyles for Party and Cocktail Nights

Get glam this season with sexy holiday hairstyles that will withstand every type of celebration, party, or festivity. As invitations begin coming in for festive soirees, Christmas dance hairstyles, family gatherings, and chic cocktail hairstyles, you will need several versatile looks to make the rounds all through the night. Whether you are looking for cute holiday hairstyles or sophisticated updos, toast to the season and ring in the holidays with style in these party-perfect 7 hot holiday hairstyles.

## 1. Glossy Waves

![Glossy Waves](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ny/xm/p9cr20fb_400x544.jpg)

Get gorgeous glossy waves for the holidays to show off your glamorous side this season. For head-turning volume and long hair holiday hairstyles that command attention, create large, soft, and smooth waves that radiate elegance. Post blow dry, prep your locks with heat protection from [ghd Hair](https://www.ghdhair.com) and curl with a large-barrel curling iron, twisting the hair around the clamp to avoid tight ringlets and create full waves. Afterwards, gently comb through the spirals with a brush or your fingers to create a soft, natural look. Finish off with a high-shine serum like [L'Oréal Paris](https://www.lorealparisusa.com) Extraordinary Oil to lock in your style for the rest of your festive evening.

Must-have essentials for creating glossy waves:

- 1.5-inch ceramic curling iron
- Thermal heat protection spray
- Shine-enhancing finishing spray

## 2. Side Sweep

![Side Sweep](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dq/fl/v8fkvx2o_600x829.jpg)

Show off your romantic side in a sultry side sweep. Tease your hair at the crown to add volume and a bit of drama. Then, like you would create a ponytail, pull half of the hair through the elastic to create a low bun style. Pin up any loose strands and cover the elastic by wrapping your hair around it. Leave some soft strands loose to frame your face for a relaxed look that is soft and romantic for cocktail hairstyles and evening events. If you prefer a more defined look, you can pair this style with [classic glossy waves](#1) for added texture and movement.

## 3. Low Textured Pony

![Low Textured Pony](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/js/qy/c7jzs6z6_420x560.jpg)

Keep your look relaxed and effortless for everyday gatherings during the holidays with a low-slung textured ponytail. For a modern, youthful, and fun way to wear the classic ponytail this season, tease hair and curl it in a way that adds texture and volume for a simple yet sexy look. Randomly curl pieces of hair with a styling tool from [TRESemmé](https://www.tresemme.com) and comb them out to add shape and playfulness. Gather hair at the nape of the neck with an elastic and conceal it by wrapping a small section of hair around the base, securing it with a bobby pin for sophisticated ease with soft feminine flair.

## 4. Relaxed Topknot

For a young, trendy, and fun new hairstyle to try for the holidays, opt for the relaxed topknot that fashion icons and celebrities turn to instead of a standard ponytail. Create your topknot in a relaxed and loose style with wispy strands around your face for a playful, pretty look that is effortlessly chic. Begin your topknot by flipping your hair over and securing it in a ponytail at the crown. Separate your hair into two sections, wrapping one around the base of the pony and securing it with a bobby pin. Then take the other section and wrap it around in the opposite direction, pinning it in place to create a perfect topknot. This style works wonderfully alongside [sleek ballerina bun](#7) techniques for formal occasions.

## 5. Side Braids

![Side Braids](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/om/sh/t02osawr_375x500.jpg)

To create holiday hair that is party perfect and dance floor ready, play around with cool side braids this season. Part your hair down the middle and begin a thin French braid on either side using the front strands, stopping near the top of your ears. To amp up your holiday style with extra glamour, tease your hair around the braids and apply hairspray to create volume and texture to rock a night out on the town.

## 6. Half up, Half down

![Half up, Half down](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gm/d6/tq0bfk95_420x560.jpg)

Amp up the holidays with hair that is tantalizingly tousled in a half up, half down style. Tease hair at the crown to create volume and sultry appeal in an effortless manner that is soft and alluring. Then, on either side of the face, pull back sections of hair from behind the ear and pin them in place at the back of the head. Allow soft layers to fall naturally around the face for a playful, romantic vibe that fits right in with [effortless topknots](#4) and relaxed party styles.

## 7. Ballerina Bun

![Ballerina Bun](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rj/xn/r957au4q_420x560.jpg)

Add an air of elegance and simple sophistication to holiday hair this season with a pretty ballerina bun. A sleek bun is ideal for formal occasions where fabulous frocks need to stand out and shine in the spotlight. Using a hair doughnut tool, slip hair into a smooth shape and secure any loose ends with bobby pins for added hold throughout your holiday soirees.

Quick tips for long-lasting bun hold:

- Use a foam hair doughnut matching your hair color
- Secure base with crisscrossed bobby pins
- Finish with strong-hold hairspray
