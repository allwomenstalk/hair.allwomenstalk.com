# 23 Stunning Unicorn Hair Ideas: Complete Guide to Pastel & Balayage Styles

Pretty pinks, beautiful blues, pastel purples, perfect platinums - these colors make up the [unicorn hair](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/mind-blowing-dyes-for-your-most-surreal-unicorn-hair-ever/) trend, and it's just as magical as it sounds. Whether you are looking for soft pastel unicorn hair, vibrant rainbow gradients, or subtle blonde hair with unicorn highlights, fantasy hair color offers endless room for creativity and self-expression. Below are 23 gorgeous photographs and styling inspirations to help you choose your next magical look.

Before taking the plunge into fantasy hair dyes, keep these key maintenance tips in mind:

- **Base Preparation:** Achieving soft pastel shades requires lifting hair to a pale blonde level.
- **Color Preservation:** Wash with cold water and sulfate-free shampoos to extend color longevity.
- **Moisture Care:** Use deep conditioning masks like [Olaplex bond building treatment](https://olaplex.com) to maintain hair health.
