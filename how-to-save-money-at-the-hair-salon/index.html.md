# Want to Save Money on Haircuts? Try These 7 Salon Saving Tips

Would you like to visit the hair salon more often but your budget doesn’t allow it? Having a great hair day can be a real boost for your hair—and your confidence—whether you want a fresh cut, a vibrant color change, or a sleek blowout. If you are looking for smart ways to practice salon saving and want to know how to save money on haircuts, these practical salon tips will help you enjoy professional treatment more often without overspending.

## 1. Refuse the Deep Condition

One easy way to save money at the hair salon is to politely decline the offer of an add-on deep conditioning treatment from your stylist. While in-salon treatments sound luxurious, you can achieve equally great results at home for a fraction of the cost. Applying natural oils like coconut oil or argan oil and letting it soak into your hair under a shower cap for 30 minutes works wonders. If you want a quick DIY routine, try these simple kitchen ingredients:

- Extra virgin coconut oil for deep moisture
- Argan oil or jojoba oil for shine and softness
- A spoonful of honey mixed with olive oil for damaged ends

This homemade deep condition is much more cost-effective, keeps your salon saving goals on track, and delivers smooth, nourished locks before your next cut.

## 2. Ask about Blowout Charge

In some salons, stylists will automatically blow-dry and style your hair to finish your look without explicitly telling you there is an additional blowout fee beforehand. This unexpected charge can easily push your visit over budget. To avoid surprise charges, always ask upfront about what is included in the base haircut price. If an extra fee applies, you can choose to skip the blowout and towel-dry or air-dry before leaving, ensuring your hair salon bill stays predictable.

| Salon Service | Average Cost | Money-Saving Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Conditioning | $25 - $50 | $3 At-Home Coconut Oil Mask |
| Blowout Finish | $35 - $60 | Air-Dry or Towel-Dry |
| Bang Trim Touch-Up | $15 - $25 | Complimentary Touch-Up or Self-Trim |

## 3. Check out a Cosmetology School

If you aren’t set on having a master stylist with decades of professional experience, visiting a local cosmetology school is a fantastic hack for how to save money on haircuts. Beauty school trainees work under the direct supervision of licensed instructors, and many schools host client nights offering cuts, color, and styling for as little as $15 to $20. Major institutions like the [Paul Mitchell Schools](https://www.paulmitchell.edu) and the [Aveda Institute](https://www.aveda.edu) provide high-quality services at student rates. You get a budget-friendly haircut, and trainees receive valuable real-world experience.

## 4. Free Bang Trims?

It is a little-known salon secret, but many salons will gladly trim your fringe or bangs for free between full haircut appointments if your original cut was done at their location. The trick is to ask your stylist about their complimentary bang trim policy before leaving your initial appointment. If you visit the same salon regularly, this complimentary maintenance keeps your style looking sharp for weeks without paying for full salon visits. You can also explore our guide on [trimming your own hair](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-save-money-at-the-hair-salon/#5) for quick touch-ups between appointments.

## 5. Trim Your Own

While cutting your own hair can feel risky, learning how to trim your own bangs at home is a great skill for salon saving. Hundreds of expert hair stylists share step-by-step tutorials on platforms like [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) demonstrating proper point-cutting techniques and hair sectioning. Always invest in proper hair shears rather than kitchen scissors, take small sections, and trim slowly to avoid accidental mistakes that require an emergency salon fix.

## 6. Low Maintenance Cut

Consider your natural hair texture and lifestyle when choosing a new hair style. Opting for a low maintenance cut—such as long layers, a textured lob, or subtle balayage—allows your hair to grow out gracefully without harsh demarcation lines. A low-maintenance haircut extends the time needed between salon appointments from 6 weeks to 10 or 12 weeks, cutting your annual hair budget nearly in half while keeping your locks stylish.

## 7. Ask for a Junior Stylist

It is standard practice for salons to offer tiered pricing based on stylist experience. Booking an appointment with a junior stylist or associate stylist often saves you up to 30% to 50% on haircuts and single-process color compared to a senior stylist or salon director. Junior stylists have completed formal training and are working to build their client base, making them a great option for routine cuts, trims, and classic styles.
