# I Tested Every Method for Easy Hair Braids: Here’s What Worked

A braid is the perfect hair style choice for just about anything. You can do a braid for the office, for a day at home, or for time out and about running errands or hanging out with friends. If you are looking for braids that are easy or wanting to learn how to master hair braiding tutorials step by step, seeing someone else demonstrate the technique can make all the difference. Whether you want quick morning styles or cute hairstyles for softball, these step-by-step video guides cover everything from basic plaits to intricate multi-strand creations. Here’s to a great hair day, every day!

Key styles covered in these easy hair tutorials include:

- **Beginner Favorites:** Classic 3-strand braids, French braids, and easy ponytail twists.
- **Intermediate & Trendy:** Fishtail braids, Dutch braids, halo braids, and waterfall styles.
- **Advanced & Statement:** Box braids, Ghana braids, multi-strand plaits, and zipper braids.

## 1. For Beginners

New to braids? This is the ultimate video for you. It’s concise and focused, making it super easy to figure out the fundamental technique for getting your braids looking neat, secure, and polished. Once you get the core method down pat, you can adapt it to create side braids, low ponytails, or build up to a [classic French braid](#3). Watch this step-by-step lesson on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) a couple of times, practice on your own hair, and you’ll be an expert braider in no time.

## 2. Fishtail Braid

Ready for a fancier braid? The fishtail braid is super trendy and offers a stylish alternative to traditional three-strand braids. While it looks complex, it actually relies on dividing hair into just two main sections and crossing thin outer strands over. Once you are comfortable with standard braiding, a little bit of practice with this video guide will have you weaving a stunning fishtail in minutes. It works beautifully for casual outings, chic dinners, or even combined into a [fishtail halo braid](#14) look.

## 3. French Braid

If you can master the classic French braid, the world of intricate hair styling opens up to you. This fundamental technique involves gathering small sections of hair as you work down the scalp, securing the style firmly in place. Mastering this timeless method serves as the foundation for endless variations, including [Double Dutch braids](#4) and top-knot accent plaits. Follow along carefully to get your hand placement right and achieve smooth, bump-free results.

## 4. Double Dutch Braid

Dutch braids are a fantastic alternative to classic French braids! While French braids weave sections over the center, a Dutch braid brings sections under, creating a bold 3D effect that pops off the head. If you are learning how to Dutch braid on someone else or on yourself, this tutorial breaks down the rhythm effortlessly. This style is perfect for active days, keeping hair secure during workouts or serving as cute hairstyles for softball. Plus, it is sleek and elegant enough to transition seamlessly into the office.

## 5. Waterfall Braid

This romantic style is a favorite across [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com) boards and wedding inspiration galleries. The waterfall braid cascades strands down while weaving an intricate band across the back of the head, giving a soft, whimsical finish. It looks sophisticated enough for a formal night out or date night, yet remains casual enough for weekend shopping or brunch with friends. The tutorial clearly demonstrates the drop-and-pick-up method so you can recreate this stunning look effortlessly.

## 6. Cornrow Tutorial

Cornrows are a classic, versatile protective style that keeps hair neat and low-maintenance. While precision sectioning and tension take practice, this clear walkthrough breaks down the underhand braiding technique step by step. Mastering self-braided cornrows gives you complete freedom over your look, whether you wear them solo, as a base for extensions, or accented with dynamic partings. Get your parting comb ready and prepare to master this essential technique!

## 7. Milkmaid Braid for Short Hair

Having short hair or a lob does not mean you have to miss out on beautiful braided styles. This clever milkmaid braid technique shows how bob-length hair can be pinned and tucked into a seamless crown wrap. By creating two quick pigtail braids and pinning them across the top, you get an instant vintage-inspired updo that stays secure all day long. Check out the strategic bobby pin placement in the tutorial to keep short layers perfectly tucked.

## 8. Two Minute Pony Braid

When you are short on time in the morning, this quick ponytail braid is a total lifesaver. Unlike severe pulled-back styles, this braid features soft volume around the crown and a relaxed texture. It is ideal for school, work, or casual errands when you need hair neatly off your face without sacrificing style. Best of all, it takes less than two minutes from start to finish!

## 9. Five Strand Braid

Ready to level up your braid game? Moving beyond the traditional three-strand weave, the five-strand braid creates a wide, ribbon-like woven effect that looks like intricate basketry. While it requires a bit of muscle memory to keep track of the outer strands, the over-under pattern is surprisingly rhythmic once you catch on. It is a stunning, unique choice for long hair that is guaranteed to gather compliments.

## 10. Eleven Strand Braid

If five strands are not enough of a challenge, an eleven-strand braid takes woven hair art to extraordinary heights! Similar to high-level multi-strand weaving like 8 strand braid hair techniques, this style turns your hair into a flat, intricate tapestry. Watch the video to appreciate the meticulous precision behind this show-stopping style.

## 11. 2 Minute Rope Braid Hairstyle

Rope braids (or twist braids) are among the easiest fancy braids you can create when time is tight. By twisting two sections of hair individually in one direction and twisting them around each other in the opposite direction, you form a sleek, rope-like spiral. This style works exceptionally well on longer hair and holds tightly throughout the day without slipping.

## 12. Frozen Elsa's Braid

Pop culture icon Elsa inspired millions with her voluminous side French braid in Frozen. If you want to recreate this voluminous, fairytale look, this tutorial breaks down how to pancake and tease the sections for maximum crown lift. Recreating an easy Elsa braid is fun for costume parties, special occasions, or whenever you want extra fairytale glam.

## 13. Warrior Braids

Fierce, striking, and empowering, warrior braids take inspiration from epic fantasy styles like Viking and Game of Thrones hair. Combining tight side cornrows with a central voluminous braid, warrior braids deliver high-impact style without requiring overly complex techniques. They even work as warrior braids for short hair when adapted into half-up accent styles!

## 14. Halo Braid

Encircling the head like an ethereal crown, the halo braid is incredibly flattering and face-framing. It strikes the perfect balance between neat updo elegance and relaxed bohemian charm. Though it appears intricate, it relies on braiding around the hairline and securing strands seamlessly with bobby pins.

## 15. Zipper Braid

Often called the zipper braid, this eye-catching technique creates a structured lattice pattern along the plait. By weaving small outer strands back and forth through the center, the finished braid resembles a zipper teeth layout. It is a show-stopping choice when you want a geometric, modern look that stands out in a crowd.

## 16. Window Braid

If you want a truly unique showcase hairstyle, the window braid offers an edgy twist on traditional plaits. Incorporating precise micro-twists and open ladder sections, this technique creates structural "windows" of hair. It takes about 15 minutes to complete and produces a striking, high-fashion finish.

## 17. MErmaid Braid

Unleash your inner sea goddess with the luxurious mermaid braid! This technique joins multiple small accent braids into one cascading main braid down the back, making long hair look immensely thick and detailed. It works best on waist-length or long hair extensions for maximum dramatic impact.

## 18. Skeleton Braid

Named for its distinctive spine-like appearance, the skeleton braid is a fun and creative style designed by top hair experts. Popularized in tutorials by [Luxy Hair](https://www.luxyhair.com), this technique uses a central spine braid surrounded by wrapped outer strands. It gives a sculptural, detailed look that holds securely all day long.

## 19. Chinese Staircase Braid

Borrowing from classic friendship bracelet knotting techniques, the Chinese staircase braid uses simple loop knots wrapped around a central ponytail base. The result is a neat, spiraled column that looks almost mathematical in its precision. It is exceptionally tidy and perfect for keeping hair out of your face during active routines.

## 20. Snake Braid

The snake braid is a clever modification of a standard 3-strand plait. After braiding normally, holding one strand still while pushing the other two strands up creates an S-shaped serpentine pattern along the hair. It is a quick, fun accent braid to add texture and movement to half-up styles.

## 21. Mohawk Pull-through Braid

For maximum volume and edgy drama, the mohawk pull-through braid delivers incredible height without needing a haircut! By linking small clear elastics together and pulling ponytail sections through each other, you achieve a thick, faux-hawk braid that won’t fall flat. Having a friend assist with parting behind the head can make styling even faster.

## 22. Box Braids

Box braids are an iconic protective style featuring clean square-shaped partings across the scalp. While installing them requires patience and precise parting, knowing how to do them at home saves significant time and money. This thorough guide covers proper scalp preparation, synthetic hair feeds, and tension control for long-lasting, healthy wear.

## 23. Ghana Braids

Ghana braids (also known as banana braids or feed-in cornrows) are a beautiful protective style that gradually increases in size from the hairline back. If you are learning how to do Ghana braids on yourself, this detailed video breaks down how to smoothly feed in extension hair for a natural, seamless transition. Everything is clearly explained so you can achieve salon-quality results at home.
