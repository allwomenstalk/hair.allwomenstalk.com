# The Elinor Haircut: Curtain Bangs and Lived\-In Waves

The “Elinor haircut” pairs soft, lash-skimming curtain bangs with long, loose, lived-in waves. It takes its name from Elinor Dashwood, played by Daisy Edgar-Jones in the new _Sense and Sensibility_, which [Focus Features lists for an October 16 theatrical release](https://www.focusfeatures.com/sense-and-sensibility). Think romantic texture with a modern, wearable cut, rather than a literal Regency hairstyle.

## What makes it the Elinor look?

[Glamour describes Edgar-Jones's hair](https://www.glamour.com/story/daisy-edgar-jones-elinor-haircut) as curtain bangs that graze the lashes, longer lengths with loose bends, and a little windblown movement. The fringe feels soft rather than perfectly set. The name is a pop-culture label for a familiar combination, not a single technical cut that every stylist will interpret the same way.

## What to say at the salon

Bring a photo of the version you like and show where you want the shortest pieces to land. A useful conversation starter is: “I'd like long lengths with soft movement and curtain bangs that open around my face. Can we work out where the fringe should fall when my hair is dry, and how to blend it into the rest?” Ask the stylist to adapt the length to your texture, density, growth pattern, and styling habits before any cutting starts.

## How to get the undone waves without heat

[Glamour suggests](https://www.glamour.com/story/daisy-edgar-jones-elinor-haircut) a light styling cream through the lengths, followed by two loose braids left in for a few hours or overnight. Release them, separate the waves with your fingers, and stop before they look too uniform. Use a small amount of product at first so the hair keeps its movement. Our [overnight hairstyle guide](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/hairstyles-you-can-sleep-in-overnight-and-into-the-morning/) and [heatless curl ideas](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/heatless-tricks-for-curling-your-hair/) offer alternatives if braids do not give you the bend you want.

For bangs, ask your stylist to demonstrate a quick morning reset on your own hair. A brush, a little water, and your usual drying method may be enough, but cowlicks and curl patterns change the approach. Glamour also suggests adding a few random curls with a heated tool; if you use heat, follow the tool's safety instructions and avoid repeated passes on one section.

## Will it work with my hair texture?

The look can be interpreted across textures, though the result will not look identical. On fine hair, your stylist may keep the layers subtle to preserve fullness. On thick hair, the conversation may focus on where the bulk sits. On wavy, curly, or coily hair, ask to judge the fringe at its _dry_ length and discuss shrinkage and how you normally style it. No face shape is disqualified; the shape and starting point of the curtain fringe can be adjusted. These are questions to take into a consultation, not universal cutting rules. You can also browse [bangs by face shape](https://hair.allwomenstalk.com/what-are-the-best-bangs-for-your-face-shape/) for visual references without treating the chart as a rulebook.

## Three ways to wear it

- **Loose and air-dried:** let your natural texture do the work, then separate a few face-framing pieces.

- **Soft waves:** use the two-braid method and brush out only enough to loosen the pattern.

- **Pinned up:** gather the lengths casually and leave the fringe and a few pieces free around the face.

The press-tour name may fade, but curtain bangs with easy movement can stay in your photo folder long after the film's opening weekend.

## FAQ

### What is the Elinor haircut?

It is the name being used for Daisy Edgar-Jones's soft curtain bangs and long, loosely waved hair during the _Sense and Sensibility_ press tour. It is a styling description rather than a newly invented cutting technique.

### What should I ask my stylist for?

Show a reference photo and ask about a soft curtain fringe blended into long, moving lengths. Discuss where the shortest bangs should sit _when dry_, your part and cowlicks, and how often you are willing to style the fringe. Let the stylist tailor the details to your hair.

### Can I style it without heat?

Yes. Glamour suggests styling cream and two loose braids left in for a few hours or overnight. Release and separate the waves gently. Your natural texture may produce a different result, so experiment with braid size and product amount rather than forcing one exact wave.

### Do I need long hair?

The press-tour version has long lengths, but its soft fringe and relaxed texture can inspire a shorter interpretation. Show your stylist both the reference and the length you want to keep. The final shape matters more than matching a celebrity's exact inches.

### When does _Sense and Sensibility_ open?

Focus Features lists the film's theatrical opening as October 16, 2026. The haircut is a press-tour-inspired look; it is not a period-accurate style requirement for enjoying the film.
